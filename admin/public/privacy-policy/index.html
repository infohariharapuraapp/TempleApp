Privacy Policy

Hariharipura Temple Devotee Mobile App

Effective Date: 21 October 2026

1. Introduction

Sreemath Hariharapura ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared and protected when devotees use the Hariharipura Temple mobile application and its associated services.

The application provides temple information, darshan videos, announcements, events, publications, Dharma Sandeham, pooja services, room booking links and other devotional features.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you register for a devotee account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Account authentication information

Account passwords are stored as protected password hashes rather than readable passwords.

2.2 Devotee Contributions

When using Dharma Sandeham, we may process questions, answers, categories, votes, reports and related moderation information.

Approved questions and answers may be visible to other devotees using the app.

2.3 Device and Notification Information

If you enable notifications, the app may register a device push-notification token to deliver temple announcements and darshan updates.

Technical information needed to operate and secure the service may also be processed, such as network and request data.

2.4 Booking and Payment Information

Pooja bookings, room reservations, donations and related payment activities may take place through separately operated temple websites or third-party payment services.

Information collected during those transactions may include names, contact information, booking details, identification information where required, and payment-related records.

Those transactions may be subject to additional terms and privacy practices applicable to the booking website or payment provider.

3. How We Use Information

We use information as necessary to:

  • Create and manage devotee accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Provide temple information and services.
  • Display and moderate Dharma Sandeham content.
  • Deliver notifications when enabled.
  • Support booking and donation services.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Promotional communications, if introduced, will be subject to applicable consent and communication requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

The application uses external infrastructure and services to operate its features. These may include:

  • Render: Backend hosting and application infrastructure.
  • Neon: PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Expo and associated push services: Mobile application functionality and push-notification delivery.
  • YouTube: Displaying temple darshan videos and related video content.
  • Temple booking websites: Pooja services, accommodation booking, donations and related transactions.

These services may process information necessary for their respective functions, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties for their own marketing.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including access controls, authentication and encrypted network connections where supported.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Account information is retained while an account remains active and as otherwise necessary for legitimate service operations and applicable legal requirements.

When a devotee deletes their app account, the associated account record and login credentials are removed from the Temple App database.

Certain records may remain where needed for legal obligations, service operations or other purposes described in this policy.

Approved Dharma Sandeham questions and answers may remain visible without the deleted account's displayed name.

Booking and payment records maintained through separate temple booking websites are managed independently of the Temple App account.

7. Account Deletion and Your Rights

Registered devotees can request deletion of their accounts directly through the mobile application.

  • Open the Hariharipura Temple App.
  • Log in to your account.
  • Open My Account.
  • Select Delete My Account.
  • Enter your password and confirm.

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting the temple support team.

View Account Deletion Instructions

Depending on applicable law, you may also request access to or correction of your personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable.

Contact support if you need assistance with personal information contained in previously published contributions.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children under 18.

Individuals under 18 should not provide personal information without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices or legal requirements change.

The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date.
