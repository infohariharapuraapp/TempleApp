Reporting a Technical Issue

Please include the following when contacting us:

  • Your device model.
  • Android or iOS version.
  • Application version, if available.
  • A brief description of the problem.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
For your security, never share your password, OTP, authentication token or payment credentials when requesting support. Remove personal information from any screenshots you provide.
