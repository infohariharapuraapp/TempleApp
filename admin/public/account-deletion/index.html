Hariharipura Temple Devotee Mobile App

Delete Your Devotee Account

Hariharipura Temple App allows registered devotees to request permanent deletion of their app accounts.

You can delete your account directly in the app, or contact the temple support team if you cannot access your account.

Option 1 — Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open the Hariharipura Temple App.
  2. Log in to your devotee account.
  3. Open My Account.
  4. Select Delete My Account.
  5. Enter your account password.
  6. Confirm the deletion request.
Once deletion is completed, your account will no longer be available for login.

Option 2 — Request Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access the mobile app, you can request account deletion through the temple's designated support contact.

Please provide the mobile number or email address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Request Account Deletion

Please mention the mobile number or email address registered with your Temple App account. The temple administration may contact you to verify account ownership before deletion.

Never include your password or OTP in the email.

What Information Is Deleted?

Deleting your account removes your devotee account and associated login credentials from the Temple App database.

App-linked device notification registrations and related account-linked records are also removed where configured.

What Information May Be Retained?

  • Dharma Sandeham questions and answers may remain visible without the deleted account's displayed name.
  • Room and pooja bookings made through the separate temple booking website are managed independently of the mobile app account.
  • Information that must be retained for legal or operational requirements may be retained under applicable policies.

If a question or answer contains personal information in its text, contact support to request a review of that content.

Need Assistance?

For account-deletion assistance, please contact the temple using its confirmed support channel.
